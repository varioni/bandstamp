The Core Debate: What Defines an "Original Member"?

Lineup history in rock 'n' roll is inherently messy. Bands swap drummers before their first tour, bring in studio session players, or add legendary long-tenured members decades down the road.

To make BandStamp deterministic and objective, we evaluate baseline rosters using three core principles:

1. The Debut Album Baseline Rule: By default, we anchor on the core musicians credited on the band's landmark debut studio album (e.g., Ronnie Wood joining The Rolling Stones in 1975 does not make him a 1964 debut baseline member).
2. The Breakthrough Core Roster Clause: When a early replacement member permanently defines the band's legacy and signature sound, they are recognized in the core baseline (e.g., Dave Grohl in Nirvana, or Travis Barker in Blink-182).
3. The Solo Mastermind Exception: Projects conceived, written, and recorded primarily as a solo auteur vehicle (e.g., Queens of the Stone Age, Foo Fighters, Nine Inch Nails, Tame Impala) baseline as 1 sole founder rather than counting hired studio session hands.

The Scoring Formula

Once the baseline roster is established, BandStamp inspects who is actually performing on stage together under the band moniker:

  • Base Score: (Present Baseline Members / Total Baseline Members) × 100
  • The Moniker Rule: Members are only counted as "present" if performing together under the official band name. Surviving members touring in side projects or solo acts do not count toward active band purity.
  • The Frontman Tax (-30%): Iconic lead vocalists carry heavy brand identity. If 2 or more original members tour without their primary lead singer, a 30% penalty is applied.
  • Last Man Standing Rule (25%): If only a single original member remains on stage and is not the primary lead vocalist, the score is set to 25% (Tier 4).
  • The Ghost Ship Rule (0%): Defunct or disbanded groups with zero core members touring under the band name evaluate to 0% (Tier 5).

The 5 Inspection Tiers

  • 100% | Tier 1: Unbroken Circle — Original/definitive founding lineup fully intact on stage.
  • 75%–99% | Tier 2: Near-Mint Vintage — Core members present with minimal lineup substitutions.
  • 50%–74% | Tier 3: Co-Founder Equity — At least half of the original core roster remains active.
  • 25%–49% | Tier 4: Solo Project in Disguise — Heavy member loss or single non-vocalist core member remaining.
  • 0%–24% | Tier 5: The Ghost Ship (Zombie Franchise) — Defunct band, no core members, or brand name operating as a ghost entity.
