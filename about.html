Lineup Purity Audit for Live Music

Methodology & Baseline Rules

The Core Debate: Baseline Lineups

Lineup purity is evaluated against a band's defining historical baseline—typically established on their debut landmark album or their classic breakthrough roster.

  • Debut Album Baseline: Default baseline consists of the original core members who recorded the debut studio album.
  • Breakthrough Roster Exception: For bands that achieved defining identity under a secondary roster (e.g., Nirvana with Dave Grohl), that roster becomes the audit baseline.
  • Solo Masterminds: Projects driven by a single auteur founder (e.g., Nine Inch Nails, Foo Fighters, Queens of the Stone Age) baseline as 1 founder.

The Scoring Formula

Base purity is calculated deterministically as (Present Baseline Members / Total Baseline Roster) * 100.

  • Frontman Penalty (-30%): Applied if two or more original members tour without their iconic lead singer.
  • Last Man Standing Rule (Hard-capped at 25%): If exactly one non-vocalist core member tours under the moniker.
  • Ghost Ship Rule (0%): If the band is defunct, dormant, or operating with zero original members on stage.
