Lineup Purity Audit for Live Music

Methodology & Inspection Rules

The Baseline Roster

Lineup purity isn't measured against whoever showed up to rehearsal last week; it’s benchmarked against a band's defining historical baseline—the classic breakthrough era that built the catalog in the first place.

  • Debut / Breakthrough Era: Baseline evaluates the classic core lineup physically responsible for the landmark records.
  • Auteur Solo Projects: Bands anchored by a singular mastermind (e.g. Nine Inch Nails, Queens of the Stone Age) baseline against that sole founder.
  • Touring Substitutes Don't Count: Hired session players, fill-ins, and secondary touring recruits are never treated as baseline members.

The Scoring Formula

Base purity is calculated deterministically as (Baseline Members Physically on Stage / Total Baseline Roster) * 100.

  • Frontman Penalty (-10%): An essential tax deducted if original members take the stage without their iconic lead singer at the mic.
  • Last Man Standing Rule (Hard-Capped at 25%): If only a single non-vocalist core member remains, the score cannot cross 25%, regardless of how loud the backing band plays.
  • Ghost Ship Rule (0%): Operating with zero original members or parading a zombie brand name guarantees an absolute zero.
  • Off-Circuit Mandate: Bands without announced upcoming tour dates automatically default to 0% with the black "Currently Not Touring" status stamp.

Scoring Tiers & Criteria

100% Tier 1: Unbroken Circle

Complete original or classic core roster intact on stage.

75% – 99% Tier 2: Near-Mint Vintage

Core members present with minimal lineup substitutions.

50% – 74% Tier 3: Co-Founder Equity

At least half of the original core roster remains active.

25% – 49% Tier 4: Solo Project in Disguise

Heavy member loss or single non-vocalist core member remaining.

0% – 24% Tier 5: The Ghost Ship (Zombie Franchise)

Defunct band, no core members, or brand name operating as a ghost entity.

OFF-CIRCUIT Currently Not Touring

Band is defunct, dormant, or has no active upcoming tour dates scheduled.
